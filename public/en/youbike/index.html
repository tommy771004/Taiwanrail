YouBike Stations

Free YouBike station finder for all 14 operating areas in Taiwan: the stations nearest to you on a map or list, with live bikes available and empty docks at each. Data comes from YouBike’s own nationwide station feed and refreshes every 60 seconds.

Open the live search →

What you can do

  • Nearby stations on a map or list, with distance
  • Rent / return modes: pins show bikes available or empty docks
  • Search station names and addresses across Taiwan, ranked by distance
  • Regular and electric-assist (YouBike 2.0E) bikes counted separately
  • Save favourite stations; a suspended station is labelled, not shown as 0 bikes

Frequently asked questions

How often does YouBike station data update?

Every 60 seconds. The data comes from YouBike’s own nationwide station feed, and the page shows the time of the reading.

Which areas are covered?

All 14 YouBike operating areas: Taipei City, New Taipei City, Taoyuan City, Hsinchu City, Hsinchu County, Hsinchu Science Park, Miaoli County, Taichung City, Chiayi City, Chiayi County, Tainan City, Kaohsiung City, Pingtung County and Taitung County.

Can I check empty docks for returning a bike?

Yes. Switch to return mode and the map pins and the list show each station’s empty docks instead of bikes.

What happens when the official feed is unreachable?

The last reading from the past 15 minutes is shown with its time. If that is also unavailable, the Taipei City open-data feed is used, which covers Taipei City only, and the page says so. If both fail no figures are shown; the site never displays estimated bike counts.
