Frequently asked questions

How long is the THSR trip from Taichung to Taipei?

The fastest direct journey takes about 43 minutes. Actual travel time varies by service and number of stops; check the live results before travelling.

How many direct THSR trains run from Taichung to Taipei?

About 93 direct trains run on weekdays and 101 at weekends. The first departs around 06:05 and the last around 23:03.

How much is the THSR fare from Taichung to Taipei?

The adult one-way fare is NT$700 for a standard reserved seat, NT$675 for a non-reserved seat, and NT$1250 for business class. Source: TDX THSR ODFare.

Is there a non-stop train from Taichung to Taipei?

Yes. The fastest service is non-stop between these stations.

Is the Taichung to Taipei timetable free to use?

Yes. Search and browsing are free and require no account. The data comes from the Taiwan Ministry of Transportation TDX public API.
