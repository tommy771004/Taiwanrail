Frequently asked questions

How long is the THSR trip from Hsinchu to Taichung?

The fastest direct journey takes about 24 minutes. Actual travel time varies by service and number of stops; check the live results before travelling.

How many direct THSR trains run from Hsinchu to Taichung?

About 59 direct trains run on weekdays and 62 at weekends. The first departs around 07:02 and the last around 23:32.

How much is the THSR fare from Hsinchu to Taichung?

The adult one-way fare is NT$410 for a standard reserved seat, NT$395 for a non-reserved seat, and NT$820 for business class. Source: TDX THSR ODFare.

Is there a non-stop train from Hsinchu to Taichung?

Yes. The fastest service is non-stop between these stations.

Is the Hsinchu to Taichung timetable free to use?

Yes. Search and browsing are free and require no account. The data comes from the Taiwan Ministry of Transportation TDX public API.

Are delays and cancellations from Hsinchu to Taichung updated live?

Not on this page. The timetable above is the recurring weekly pattern, as of 2026-08-17. Opening the live timetable retrieves delay minutes from TDX LiveBoard and cancellation notices from TDX Alert.
