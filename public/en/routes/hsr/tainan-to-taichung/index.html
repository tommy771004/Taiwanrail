Frequently asked questions

How long is the THSR trip from Tainan to Taichung?

The fastest direct journey takes about 35 minutes. Actual travel time varies by service and number of stops; check the live results before travelling.

How many direct THSR trains run from Tainan to Taichung?

About 71 direct trains run on weekdays and 73 at weekends. The first departs around 06:03 and the last around 23:08.

How much is the THSR fare from Tainan to Taichung?

The adult one-way fare is NT$650 for a standard reserved seat, NT$630 for a non-reserved seat, and NT$1180 for business class. Source: TDX THSR ODFare.

Is there a non-stop train from Tainan to Taichung?

Yes. The fastest service is non-stop between these stations.

Is the Tainan to Taichung timetable free to use?

Yes. Search and browsing are free and require no account. The data comes from the Taiwan Ministry of Transportation TDX public API.

Are delays and cancellations from Tainan to Taichung updated live?

Not on this page. The timetable above is the recurring weekly pattern, as of 2026-08-17. Opening the live timetable retrieves delay minutes from TDX LiveBoard and cancellation notices from TDX Alert.
