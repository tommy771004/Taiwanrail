Frequently asked questions

How long is the THSR trip from Taoyuan to Taichung?

The fastest direct journey takes about 30 minutes. Actual travel time varies by service and number of stops; check the live results before travelling.

How many direct THSR trains run from Taoyuan to Taichung?

About 67 direct trains run on weekdays and 71 at weekends. The first departs around 06:49 and the last around 23:21.

How much is the THSR fare from Taoyuan to Taichung?

The adult one-way fare is NT$540 for a standard reserved seat, NT$520 for a non-reserved seat, and NT$1010 for business class. Source: TDX THSR ODFare.

Is there a non-stop train from Taoyuan to Taichung?

Yes. The fastest service is non-stop between these stations.

Is the Taoyuan to Taichung timetable free to use?

Yes. Search and browsing are free and require no account. The data comes from the Taiwan Ministry of Transportation TDX public API.
