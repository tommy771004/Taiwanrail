Frequently asked questions

How long is the THSR trip from Chiayi to Tainan?

The fastest direct journey takes about 16 minutes. Actual travel time varies by service and number of stops; check the live results before travelling.

How many direct THSR trains run from Chiayi to Tainan?

About 56 direct trains run on weekdays and 59 at weekends. The first departs around 06:59 and the last around 23:30.

How much is the THSR fare from Chiayi to Tainan?

The adult one-way fare is NT$280 for a standard reserved seat, NT$270 for a non-reserved seat, and NT$620 for business class. Source: TDX THSR ODFare.

Is there a non-stop train from Chiayi to Tainan?

Yes. The fastest service is non-stop between these stations.

Is the Chiayi to Tainan timetable free to use?

Yes. Search and browsing are free and require no account. The data comes from the Taiwan Ministry of Transportation TDX public API.

Are delays and cancellations from Chiayi to Tainan updated live?

Not on this page. The timetable above is the recurring weekly pattern, as of 2026-08-17. Opening the live timetable retrieves delay minutes from TDX LiveBoard and cancellation notices from TDX Alert.
