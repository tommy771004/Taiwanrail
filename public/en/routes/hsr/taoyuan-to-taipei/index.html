Frequently asked questions

How long is the THSR trip from Taoyuan to Taipei?

The fastest direct journey takes about 16 minutes. Actual travel time varies by service and number of stops; check the live results before travelling.

How many direct THSR trains run from Taoyuan to Taipei?

About 62 direct trains run on weekdays and 70 at weekends. The first departs around 06:49 and the last around 23:40.

How much is the THSR fare from Taoyuan to Taipei?

The adult one-way fare is NT$160 for a standard reserved seat, NT$155 for a non-reserved seat, and NT$440 for business class. Source: TDX THSR ODFare.

Is there a non-stop train from Taoyuan to Taipei?

Yes. The fastest service is non-stop between these stations.

Is the Taoyuan to Taipei timetable free to use?

Yes. Search and browsing are free and require no account. The data comes from the Taiwan Ministry of Transportation TDX public API.

Are delays and cancellations from Taoyuan to Taipei updated live?

Not on this page. The timetable above is the recurring weekly pattern, as of 2026-08-17. Opening the live timetable retrieves delay minutes from TDX LiveBoard and cancellation notices from TDX Alert.
