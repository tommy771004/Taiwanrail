Frequently asked questions

How long is the THSR trip from Taipei to Taoyuan?

The fastest direct journey takes about 17 minutes. Actual travel time varies by service and number of stops; check the live results before travelling.

How many direct THSR trains run from Taipei to Taoyuan?

About 67 direct trains run on weekdays and 71 at weekends. The first departs around 06:26 and the last around 23:00.

How much is the THSR fare from Taipei to Taoyuan?

The adult one-way fare is NT$160 for a standard reserved seat, NT$155 for a non-reserved seat, and NT$440 for business class. Source: TDX THSR ODFare.

Is there a non-stop train from Taipei to Taoyuan?

Yes. The fastest service is non-stop between these stations.

Is the Taipei to Taoyuan timetable free to use?

Yes. Search and browsing are free and require no account. The data comes from the Taiwan Ministry of Transportation TDX public API.

Are delays and cancellations from Taipei to Taoyuan updated live?

Not on this page. The timetable above is the recurring weekly pattern, as of 2026-08-17. Opening the live timetable retrieves delay minutes from TDX LiveBoard and cancellation notices from TDX Alert.
