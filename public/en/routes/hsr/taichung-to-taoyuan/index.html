Frequently asked questions

How long is the THSR trip from Taichung to Taoyuan?

The fastest direct journey takes about 30 minutes. Actual travel time varies by service and number of stops; check the live results before travelling.

How many direct THSR trains run from Taichung to Taoyuan?

About 62 direct trains run on weekdays and 70 at weekends. The first departs around 06:05 and the last around 22:59.

How much is the THSR fare from Taichung to Taoyuan?

The adult one-way fare is NT$540 for a standard reserved seat, NT$520 for a non-reserved seat, and NT$1010 for business class. Source: TDX THSR ODFare.

Is there a non-stop train from Taichung to Taoyuan?

Yes. The fastest service is non-stop between these stations.

Is the Taichung to Taoyuan timetable free to use?

Yes. Search and browsing are free and require no account. The data comes from the Taiwan Ministry of Transportation TDX public API.

Are delays and cancellations from Taichung to Taoyuan updated live?

Not on this page. The timetable above is the recurring weekly pattern, as of 2026-08-17. Opening the live timetable retrieves delay minutes from TDX LiveBoard and cancellation notices from TDX Alert.
