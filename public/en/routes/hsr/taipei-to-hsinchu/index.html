Frequently asked questions

How long is the THSR trip from Taipei to Hsinchu?

The fastest direct journey takes about 29 minutes. Actual travel time varies by service and number of stops; check the live results before travelling.

How many direct THSR trains run from Taipei to Hsinchu?

About 59 direct trains run on weekdays and 62 at weekends. The first departs around 06:26 and the last around 23:00.

How much is the THSR fare from Taipei to Hsinchu?

The adult one-way fare is NT$290 for a standard reserved seat, NT$280 for a non-reserved seat, and NT$640 for business class. Source: TDX THSR ODFare.

Does the fastest train from Taipei to Hsinchu make intermediate stops?

Is the Taipei to Hsinchu timetable free to use?

Yes. Search and browsing are free and require no account. The data comes from the Taiwan Ministry of Transportation TDX public API.
