Frequently asked questions

Which cities’ buses are covered?

City buses in Taipei, New Taipei, Taoyuan, Taichung and Kaohsiung. Stop and route data comes from the Ministry of Transportation TDX platform and is refreshed weekly.

How often do bus arrival times update?

Every 60 seconds while a stop or route page is open. The figures are TDX estimated arrival times, shown in whole minutes; under one minute reads “Arriving”.

What do “Last bus left” and “No service today” mean?

They are stop statuses reported by TDX: “Last bus left”, “No service today” and “Not stopping” (skipped for traffic control). A bus that has not set off yet shows its scheduled time (“Sched.”) when TDX provides one; anything else shows “No data”. The site never estimates an arrival time itself.

Do I need to share my location?

No. With location allowed the nearest stops appear straight away; without it you can search by stop name or route name.
